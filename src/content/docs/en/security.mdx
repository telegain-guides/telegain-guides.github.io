<Aside type="caution" title="No promises">
No tool can guarantee that a Telegram account stays intact. The platform decides on restrictions,
and there is no universally “safe” pace — it depends on the account's age, its history and the target
itself.
</Aside>

## What the panel does

**Spread pauses.** The pace profile sets the gaps between actions, and every pause differs slightly
from the previous one — consecutive actions never repeat the same interval.

**FloodWait handling.** If Telegram asks to wait, the thread pauses instead of pushing the account
further. In inviting there is a choice: wait out the delay and continue, or stop that account
for the current job.

**Daily limits and a night break.** Configured separately from pace.

**Stopping before sending.** Some actions are stopped by protection before Telegram is contacted —
in the results this appears as a skip rather than an error.

**Thread isolation.** Pause and stop apply to a single account; the neighbours keep working.

## What depends on the operator

- **A proxy on every account** — before the first action in Telegram, not after.
- **Warmup before working tasks** — duration matched to the account's age.
- **Sensible volume** — putting the whole limit on one account instead of spreading it across
  the fleet remains the most common cause of problems.
- **A filled-in profile** — the panel does not set the name, avatar and bio for you.
- **Varied text** — an identical message from every account is obvious to readers and anti-spam alike.

## Data in this documentation

The screenshots in this knowledge base were taken on demonstration data: there are no real phone
numbers, sessions, proxy credentials or client usernames in frame.

## What warmup does not do

Warmup does not lift restrictions already imposed and does not unblock an account. An account under
restriction has to be checked and recovered first — see the
[warmup guide](/en/modules/account-warmup/).
